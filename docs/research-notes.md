# Research Notes

Use this file to collect papers, datasets, experimental observations, and modeling decisions.

## Representative Literature To Track

- Lithium metal battery reviews covering dendrite growth, dead lithium, SEI
  instability, Coulombic efficiency, and short-circuit risk.
- Lithium metal or anode-free cell datasets with cycle logs, CE, current
  density, areal capacity, electrolyte formulation, and voltage profiles.
- Severson et al. and Attia et al. as method references only, not as final LMB
  evidence.
- Reviews on SOH/RUL estimation and battery management systems, clearly marked
  by chemistry and cell type.

## Open Questions

- Which lithium metal cell format will be used first: Li-metal excess,
  anode-free, Li||Cu half cell, or full cell?
- What is the end-of-life criterion: capacity retention, CE instability,
  overpotential/polarization threshold, soft-short warning, or
  application-specific failure?
- Which lithium-metal-specific fields are available from the lab: CE,
  charge/discharge capacity, current density, areal capacity, N/P ratio,
  electrolyte, pressure, EIS, or voltage profile?
- Which experimental devices have APIs, and which require GUI automation?
- What is the minimum safe action set for early strategy optimization experiments?
